# Images

## `photos/`
Ethiopian food photos from Wikimedia Commons under free licences (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA).
Authors and licences are listed under **Photo credits** in the footer of every page. Keep that list if you keep the photos.

## Add your own photos
- `rooms.jpg`: a photo of a guest room. It appears as the Rooms page banner and on the home-page Rooms card; until it exists a bed icon shows.
- `garden.jpg`: a photo of the Selam Village garden. It appears automatically as the Garden & Events page banner and in the garden tile; until it exists a green pattern shows.
- To swap any food photo for one of the restaurant's own, overwrite the file in `photos/` with the same name (and remove its line from the Photo credits in each page footer).
