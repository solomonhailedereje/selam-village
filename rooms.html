Rooms at Selam Village

Stay a night or a week in the heart of Giorgis. Our guest rooms are right beside the restaurant and garden. Wake up to fresh coffee and Ethiopian hospitality.

    Quiet, clean rooms

    Comfortable rooms close to our garden, away from the street noise.

    Restaurant, bar & coffee on site

    Central location

    In Giorgis, next to Wube Berha Apartment, close to Piassa and the city centre.

    Perfect for event guests
