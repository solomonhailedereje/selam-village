Our Story

A village table, set since 1983

Selam Village began with a simple promise: to cook the way our mothers and grandmothers did — patiently, generously, and with the freshest spices we could find. "Selam" means peace, and for more than forty years our home has been a place of peace, conversation and shared plates.

Our berbere is still blended in-house, our niter kibbeh still clarified slowly, and our injera still fermented the traditional way from teff. What has grown is our family — the guests, weddings and celebrations we have had the honour of feeding across generations.

"Food eaten together from one plate makes one family."
  1. 1983Selam Village opens its doors with a small kitchen and a handful of tables.
  2. 1990sWord spreads — we begin catering weddings, holidays and community gatherings.
  3. 2000sA larger dining room and our signature traditional coffee ceremony.
  4. TodayA second generation carries the recipes forward — from intimate dinners to events of hundreds.
