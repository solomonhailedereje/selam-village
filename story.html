Our Story

Our Story

From butchery to village, since 1988

Our story began in 1988 as Selam Butchery & Grocery, a neighbourhood shop known for fresh, carefully selected ox and goat meat. Our customers trusted us, and before long we opened a second shop: Selam Butchery & Grocery 2.

In 2016 we moved and became Selam Village: a restaurant with a big garden, a traditional hall and guest rooms. Our butchery roots are the reason our tire siga, kitfo and tibs taste the way they do, and we still supply quality meat to hotels and supermarkets.

"Food eaten together from one plate makes one family."
  1. 1988Selam Butchery & Grocery is founded, bringing fresh ox and goat meat to our neighbourhood.
  2. ExpansionWe open our second shop, Selam Butchery & Grocery 2.
  3. 2016We move and become Selam Village: restaurant, garden, events and catering in one place.
